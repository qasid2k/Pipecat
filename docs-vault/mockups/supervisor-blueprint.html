Suggested build order

Each step is one slice: built, tested, and checked live on the VM before the next. Steps 1 and 2 fix what the numbers mean before anything is built on them.

1 · NextHonest numbersThe resolution rule ("Resolved by the AI" and "Unclear"), the business time zone, and masked caller numbers by default. The rules every later page uses.
2Insights page + today's numbers on LiveOne summary query over the calls table. Numbers click through to Calls, filtered.
3Couldn't get throughRead Asterisk's call records (CDR) so busy turn-aways show up in Live, Calls and Insights.
4Calls upgrade + exportOutcome chips, quick filters, the caller's first line, and CSV export.
5System healthProvider response times and errors (pairs with IMP-008), the greeting-wait figures, audio and machines. Engineering numbers move off the supervisor pages.
6Agents: stats + table viewPer-agent numbers, and the table view for large teams.
7Login, then office-network accessIMP-010. Unlocks full numbers, Wallboard mode, and everything that acts.
8Listen in and transferSupervisor actions on a live call, using Asterisk's listen and transfer features.
9Settings + notificationsConfig without YAML, alert emails or Slack, the weekly summary, and privacy settings.

Sources used for the layout choices: real-time and historical views kept apart, and only the few numbers that need action on the live screen (CustomerThink, Voiso); AI-agent metrics such as containment, escalation reasons and time to first word, with every number drilling down to the calls behind it (Hamming, Parloa).
